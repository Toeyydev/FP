Frequently asked questions

Are entrance tickets included?

Inclusions vary by tour. Each tour page lists known admission costs, and the selected Bókun option shows the final inclusions and price before payment.

Is this a private or shared tour?

The Grand Palace, Wat Pho & Wat Arun half-day tour is a join-in tour, so you may share your guide with other travelers on the same departure. If you would like a guide just for your group, book a private tour instead.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

The Chinatown food tour is not suitable for vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, nut-allergy, or other restricted diets. Many dishes contain pork or chicken, and stalls cannot guarantee separation from allergens or cross-contamination.

What is the weather like in Bangkok?

Bangkok is warm all year: cool and dry from November to February, hottest from March to May, and rainy from June to October, usually with short afternoon showers. Wear light, breathable clothing, bring water, and carry an umbrella in the rainy season.

What happens in heavy rain?

Tours normally continue in light rain. If conditions affect safety or access, the operations team will contact booked guests using the details supplied at checkout.

Are private tours available?

Yes. Private routes can be adapted for families, photographers, repeat visitors, mobility needs, and travelers with limited time.
