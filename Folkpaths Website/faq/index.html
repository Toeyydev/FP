Frequently asked questions

Are entrance tickets included?

Inclusions vary by tour. The secure checkout shows the final inclusions and price before payment. Temple clothing requirements and any admission fees are also stated on each tour page.

How large are the groups?

Folkpaths specializes in small-group experiences. The exact capacity appears with live availability for your selected date.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

The Chinatown food tour is not suitable for vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, nut-allergy, or other restricted diets. Many dishes contain pork or chicken, and stalls cannot guarantee separation from allergens or cross-contamination.

What happens in heavy rain?

Tours normally continue in light rain. If conditions affect safety or access, the operations team will contact booked guests using the details supplied at checkout.

Are private tours available?

Yes. Private routes can be adapted for families, photographers, repeat visitors, mobility needs, and travelers with limited time.

Ready to choose?

Live dates, capacity, final prices, and payment options appear in the secure checkout.
