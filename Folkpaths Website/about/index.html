Why Folkpaths exists

Bangkok can feel overwhelming when every street contains several histories at once. Folkpaths guides translate that density into a route you can follow, while leaving room for questions, weather, temple etiquette, and the unexpected moments that make a walk memorable.

The company focuses on small-group and private experiences across cultural landmarks, food streets, markets, and neighborhoods.

Context changes a place from something you photograph into something you remember.
