A guided experience through Wat Phra Kaew and the Grand Palace, Wat Pho, and Wat Arun—with the stories and customs that connect them.

Highlights

  • Connect three of Bangkok’s defining royal and riverside landmarks in one route.
  • Explore with a licensed English-speaking guide.
  • Cross the Chao Phraya River by local ferry.

Itinerary

  1. Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew
  2. Wat Pho and the Reclining Buddha
  3. River ferry crossing
  4. Wat Arun
  5. Return by ferry and finish at Tha Tien

Included

  • Licensed professional English-speaking guide.
  • Round-trip Chao Phraya River ferry crossing.
  • Travel insurance.
  • One bottle of water per person.

Extra costs

Temple admission is not included. Foreign-adult entry is Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew ฿500, Wat Pho ฿300, and Wat Arun ฿200: ฿1,000 total per person. Venue prices can change; recheck before your tour.

Meeting point

The Gate Grand Palace Restaurant, Na Phra Lan Road, Phra Borom Maha Ratchawang, Phra Nakhon, Bangkok. Open in Google Maps.

Allow extra time for Old Town traffic. The nearest practical public-transport approach is MRT Sanam Chai followed by a short taxi, bus, or walk; check your booking email for the exact meeting instructions.

End point

The tour returns to Tha Tien after the Wat Arun visit and ferry crossing.

What to bring

  • Clothing that covers shoulders and ankles; scarves are not accepted as shoulder covering at the Grand Palace.
  • Comfortable shoes, sun protection, an umbrella, and drinking water.
  • ฿1,000 per foreign adult for temple entry.
