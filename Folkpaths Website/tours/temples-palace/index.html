Temples Cultural Walk

Bangkok: Half-Day Guided: Grand Palace, Wat Pho & Wat Arun

A guided experience through Wat Phra Kaew and the Grand Palace, Wat Pho, and Wat Arun—with the stories and customs that connect them.

A route that connects the landmarks

The Grand Palace, Wat Pho, and Wat Arun are often visited as isolated sights. This route joins them into one narrative, from royal Bangkok and the Emerald Buddha to temple medicine, daily worship, and the river that shaped the city.

Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew

Read the architecture and murals with context on royal tradition, Buddhism, and respectful behavior inside Thailand’s most important ceremonial complex.

Wat Pho

Meet the Reclining Buddha and learn why the temple is also associated with education, traditional medicine, and massage.

Across the Chao Phraya to Wat Arun

Take the local ferry to the Temple of Dawn and finish with the riverside view that ties old Bangkok together.
