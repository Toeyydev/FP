Eat Like a Local: Bangkok Chinatown Food Tour (10+Tastings)

  • About 2.5 hours
  • 10+ tastings
  • Guided in English
  • Walking tour
  • From ฿1,300 per person

Skip the guesswork and eat your way through Yaowarat with someone who can explain what is in the bowl, why it belongs here, and what to try next.

Come hungry and curious

Bangkok’s Chinatown is fast, crowded, and rewarding. Your guide builds a tasting route across stalls and shophouses, using food to unpack migration, trade, family businesses, and Thai-Chinese culture.

What you may taste

The exact stops follow opening days and what is good that evening. Expect a varied sequence of savory dishes, snacks, sweets, and drinks rather than one large dinner. Many dishes contain pork or chicken.

Dietary restrictions

This tour is not suitable for vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, nut-allergy, or other restricted diets. Ingredients and preparation methods vary between stalls, and separation from allergens or cross-contamination cannot be guaranteed.

Why a guide helps

Menus, queues, and ordering styles can be difficult to decode at peak hour. A local guide handles the practical parts while giving you enough knowledge to return independently.

Not included

  • Transport to and from the meeting point and personal expenses.
  • Guide tips are optional and not included in the tour price.
