10+ tastings are included. Skip the guesswork and eat your way through Yaowarat with someone who can explain what is in the bowl, why it belongs here, and what to try next.

Highlights

  • Try ten or more tastings across Yaowarat stalls and shophouses.
  • Learn how migration, trade, and family businesses shaped Bangkok Chinatown.
  • Get practical help ordering in one of Bangkok’s busiest food districts.

Itinerary

The exact tasting route follows opening days and what is good that evening. Expect a varied sequence of savory dishes, snacks, sweets, and drinks rather than one large dinner.

Tour questions

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

The Chinatown food tour is not suitable for vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, nut-allergy, or other restricted diets. Many dishes contain pork or chicken, and stalls cannot guarantee separation from allergens or cross-contamination.

What is the weather like in Bangkok?

Bangkok is warm all year: cool and dry from November to February, hottest from March to May, and rainy from June to October, usually with short afternoon showers. Wear light, breathable clothing, bring water, and carry an umbrella in the rainy season.
