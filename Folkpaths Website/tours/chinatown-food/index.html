Tour questions

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

The Chinatown food tour is not suitable for vegetarian, vegan, halal, gluten-free, nut-allergy, or other restricted diets. Many dishes contain pork or chicken, and stalls cannot guarantee separation from allergens or cross-contamination.

What is the weather like in Bangkok?

Bangkok is warm all year: cool and dry from November to February, hottest from March to May, and rainy from June to October, usually with short afternoon showers. Wear light, breathable clothing, bring water, and carry an umbrella in the rainy season.
