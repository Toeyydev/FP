See the Grand Palace, Wat Pho and Wat Arun in the morning, then meet your food guide in Chinatown for 10+ tastings in the evening. The 6.5 hours of guided activities are split by a free afternoon.

Highlights

  • Visit the Emerald Buddha at Wat Phra Kaew and the Reclining Buddha at Wat Pho.
  • Cross the Chao Phraya River to see Wat Arun.
  • Try Thai and Thai-Chinese specialties on an evening walk through Yaowarat.

Itinerary

  1. Morning: Grand Palace and Wat Phra Kaew, Wat Pho, and Wat Arun.
  2. Afternoon: free time and independent travel to Chinatown.
  3. Evening: meet at MRT Wat Mangkon, Exit 1, for the food walk.

Included

  • English-speaking guides for both walks.
  • 10+ food tastings in Chinatown.

Extra costs

  • Admission to the Grand Palace, Wat Pho and Wat Arun.
  • Hotel pickup and drop-off, travel between tours, and food and drinks outside the included tastings.

Meeting point

Check your booking confirmation for the morning meeting point. For the evening walk, meet at MRT Wat Mangkon, Exit 1. Arrive 15 minutes early for each tour.

End point

The evening walk ends in Chinatown. Ask your guide for directions to your next stop.

What to bring

  • Clothes that cover your shoulders and ankles, comfortable walking shoes, and Thai baht for admission.
  • See the temple dress code and packing list.
  • Contact Folkpaths before booking if you have food allergies or dietary requirements. Substitutions depend on the vendors.
