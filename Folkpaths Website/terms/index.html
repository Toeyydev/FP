Terms & Conditions

Clear booking terms for Folkpaths tours and experiences.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

These terms apply when you book an activity supplied by Folkpaths Co., Ltd. Product-specific information displayed at checkout and on your booking confirmation forms part of your agreement. If a product-specific term differs from these general terms, the term displayed for your selected booking applies.

1. Booking and confirmation

A booking is confirmed only when the booking platform issues a confirmation or voucher. Please check the activity, date, time, participant details, meeting point, selected option, and contact information immediately. Contact the booking channel promptly if anything is incorrect.

2. Prices and payment

The checkout displays the price and currency payable for the selected option. Items described as excluded—including admission fees, meals, transport, personal expenses, and optional guide tips—must be paid separately. Payments are processed through the secure payment providers enabled at checkout; their payment terms also apply.

3. Cancellations and refunds

Cancellation terms are rate-specific and displayed beside the selected rate at checkout and on the confirmation. Some rates may be non-refundable. Those product-specific terms control the booking. If the checkout and product description appear inconsistent, contact Folkpaths before payment. Approved refunds are returned through the original booking channel and payment method.

4. Changes

Requests to change the date, time, option, or participants are subject to availability and must be made through the channel used to book. A request is not confirmed until written confirmation is issued. Any price difference applicable to the new booking may be charged.

5. Arrival, meeting points and no-shows

Guests are responsible for reaching the confirmed meeting point on time. Bangkok traffic and venue queues should be considered when planning travel. If you may be late, contact the operator through WhatsApp as soon as possible. Late admission cannot be guaranteed, and a missed activity may be treated as a no-show.

6. Weather, closures and operational changes

Activities normally operate in sun and rain. Folkpaths may reasonably change the route, sequence, transport, guide, timing, or included stop when necessary for safety, weather, official closures, religious or royal ceremonies, government action, or circumstances outside reasonable control. If Folkpaths cancels an activity and cannot provide a suitable alternative, the booking will be refunded through the original booking channel.

7. Participant responsibilities

Guests must review the activity’s walking level, accessibility, health guidance, age rules, dress code, and prohibited items before booking. Guests must follow guide instructions, venue rules, and Thai law. Folkpaths or a venue may refuse participation when conduct, dress, health, intoxication, safety, or legal requirements are not met.

8. Children, accessibility and special requirements

Any age, child-price, stroller, wheelchair, dietary, or mobility conditions shown for the activity apply. Tell Folkpaths about relevant requirements before booking so suitability can be assessed. Not all Bangkok routes or heritage sites are step-free.

9. Personal belongings and conduct

Guests remain responsible for passports, tickets, money, phones, and other personal property. Respectful behavior is required at temples, palaces, markets, communities, and food venues.

10. Booking platforms

When booking through Folkpaths, GetYourGuide, Viator, or another marketplace, the applicable checkout, payment, cancellation, privacy, and customer-service terms also apply. Changes and refunds should normally be requested through the original booking channel.
