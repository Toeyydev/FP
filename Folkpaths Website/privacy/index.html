Privacy & Cookie Notice

What the website collects, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Website analytics and advertising

Optional Google Analytics and Google Ads tags remain disabled until you accept optional cookies. If accepted, these tools may process device, browser, page, referral, and interaction information to measure website use and advertising performance. You can change your choice by clearing this site’s storage and revisiting the site.

Bookings

Live availability, checkout, and payment are provided through Bókun and the payment methods enabled there. Their privacy terms also apply when you continue to checkout.

Verified direct-booking reviews

For review verification, Folkpaths processes the complete booking confirmation code and booking email solely to confirm a matching, completed booking and prevent duplicate reviews. The public review may contain only the display name, rating, review text, tour name, and tour month you agreed to publish. Booking codes and email addresses are not published. Submissions are moderated before publication.

Security and retention

Review identifiers are stored as one-way hashes. Access attempts may be rate-limited using a short-lived one-way network identifier. Pending or rejected review data should be deleted under the operator’s retention schedule; published reviews are retained until withdrawn or no longer needed.

Your choices and contact

You may ask to access, correct, or remove a direct-booking review by contacting admin@folkpaths.com. Include enough information to locate the review, but do not send payment-card details.
