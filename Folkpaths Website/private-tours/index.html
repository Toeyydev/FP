Bangkok, your way.

A private guide, a sensible pace, and a route shaped around what your group genuinely wants to understand.

Useful flexibility, not a blank itinerary

Start from a proven Folkpaths route, then adapt the emphasis, pace, and practical details. Private tours work especially well for families, photographers, repeat visitors, travelers with mobility considerations, and short Bangkok stays.

Culture and temples

Focus on Buddhist practice, royal history, architecture, or fewer sites with deeper explanation.

Food and neighborhoods

Build around morning markets, Chinatown, old shophouses, or places where food explains how a neighborhood changed.

Accessibility and pace

Share mobility, sensory, dietary, or rest needs before confirmation. The team can then recommend what is realistic rather than promising a route that will not work.

How planning works

  1. Choose a preferred theme and date.
  2. Share group size, timing, interests, and practical needs directly with the operator on WhatsApp.
  3. Folkpaths confirms the route and sends the correct booking option.

Not included

  • Items not listed in the confirmed private itinerary and personal expenses.
  • Guide tips are optional and not included in the tour price.

Before the tour

  • If you may be late, contact the operator through WhatsApp.
  • Read the Folkpaths Terms & Conditions.
